Delete Your Account – Your Salary Calculator

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

Option 1: Delete Inside the App

Open Your Salary Calculator, open the three-dot menu (⋮), tap Delete Account, and confirm your request.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Web / Email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us at afsarraza605@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request - Your Salary Calculator".

What Data Is Deleted & Retention

Once verified, your account profile and associated app data will be permanently deleted within 30 days, except records required to be retained for legal, security, or compliance purposes.
